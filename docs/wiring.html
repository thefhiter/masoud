Finding the pins on your amp

PAM8403 boards come in a few versions and print the same pins with different names. Match yours here.

Input (from the Arduino)

L R plus a ground marked G, ⏚ or GND. Sometimes LIN / RIN. Usually a row of 3 small pins on one edge.

Power

+5V, VCC or + takes the Uno's 5V. GND or − takes the Uno's GND. Usually 2 pins, sometimes marked in red.

Speakers

L+ L− and R+ R−, or LOUT+ LOUT−, ROUT+ ROUT−. On boards with 2 screw terminals, the one marked L is speaker 1 and the one marked R is speaker 2.

If your board looks different, send a photo of it in the chat and the pins will be marked for you.

Before you plug in the USB

  1. Screen VCC is on 3.3V, not 5V.
  2. No speaker wire touches GND or the Arduino.
  3. D9 and D10 only go to the screen (DC and LCD_CS).
  4. Bare wire ends don't touch each other, especially under the amp.
