First run

Six steps.
No app.

Setup happens on his face and in the phone's browser. He makes his own Wi-Fi, so it works on any phone with nothing to install.

  1. Plug in

    USB-C, no battery. His eyes open, he says hello and shows a QR code.

    "Aslema! Ena Masoud."

  2. Scan the QR

    The phone joins his setup Wi-Fi, Masoud-4F2A, and the setup page opens by itself.

  3. Pick a language

    Derja, Français, English or العربية. Voice and captions switch at once.

  4. Join home Wi-Fi

    Choose the network, type the password. He confirms with a happy face.

  5. Choose his mind

    Local, Cloud or Pet mode. Change it any time from the same page.

  6. Meet him

    Type your name, say "Ya Masoud!" to test the wake word, then shake him once.

    "Dawkha! Mrigel, nharek mas3oud, Sami."

Before you sell the first one

Check the name is free in the INNORPI and WIPO Global Brand databases, then register "Masoud" and the logo at INNORPI in classes 9, 28 and 42.

Grab the handles and domain: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and a .tn or .com.

Set up the company (SUARL or SARL) and apply for the Startup Act label.

Check the rules for a Wi-Fi device: radio type approval in Tunisia, CE marking for Europe.

Write a plain privacy notice for Cloud mind, and check your obligations with the INPDP.

Ask a Tunisian business lawyer to review this list. This book is a plan, not legal advice.
